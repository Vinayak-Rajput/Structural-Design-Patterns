Smart Home "Movie Night" System

Triggering a home theater setup requires interacting with
multiple distinct subsystems: dimming SmartLights, powering
on the Projector, switching the SoundSystem to surround mode,
and launching the StreamingDevice.

Task: Create a HomeTheaterFacade class with high-level
watchMovie(String title) and endMovie() methods that coordinate
all underlying subsystems behind a simple interface.
